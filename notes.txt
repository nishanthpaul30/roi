Welcome to the GenAI Usage & ROI Dashboard — an intelligence platform built to
go beyond traditional reporting. What sets it apart from standard dashboards is
that it doesn't just display data: it thinks about it and provides actionable
recommendations.

Most dashboards stop at visibility — charts, tables, filters. This one goes a
step further with a built-in Strategic Inference Engine. Ingesting raw usage
telemetry directly from provided CSV data sources — across teams, adopted AI
tools, and business units — it automatically surfaces the handful of findings
that actually matter to leadership: which licenses are sitting completely
dormant, which teams are quietly over-relying on non-billable AI spend, where
project billability is slipping, who the habitual power users are, and where
cost concentration creates risk.

Each of these isn't just a flag — it comes with a quantified dollar impact and a
specific, ready-to-execute recommendation. For example, the engine can pinpoint
immediately recoverable savings from dormant licenses, paired with a direct
reclamation action — not a suggestion to 'go investigate,' but a decision
already packaged for approval.

Ultimately, this dashboard doesn't just answer 'what happened with AI spend.'
It answers 'what should we do about it' — turning raw telemetry into strategic,
dollar-backed action items, automatically, every time the data updates.
